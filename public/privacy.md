# PRG32 Multiplayer Server privacy notice template

The controller operating this server must replace this template with its legal
identity, contacts, purposes, legal bases, recipients, hosting location, and
supervisory authority before inviting players.

The service processes a keyed pseudonymous device identifier, client platform,
room signature, connection times, message byte counts, and operational event
types. It does not intentionally store IP addresses, names, raw MAC addresses,
advertising identifiers, game coordinates, inputs, or message bodies. Data is
used to operate and secure multiplayer sessions and produce aggregate service
statistics. The default retention period is 30 days.

Players may ask the controller for access, export, correction, restriction, or
erasure using the controller contact supplied here. Pseudonymous identifiers
may require the player to provide the public device ID shown by their client.
Data is not sold. International transfers, processors, and the applicable
legal basis must be documented by the deployment operator.
