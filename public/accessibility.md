# Accessibility statement

**[Operator name]** wants everyone to be able to use the PRG32 multiplayer
status pages and admin console. This statement applies to the web pages served
by this server.

## Compliance status

These pages aim to conform to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG)
2.2 at level AA** and to the corresponding requirements of **EN 301 549**.
They were last reviewed on [date] by [self-assessment or third party].

## What we did

- Semantic structure with landmarks, one main heading per page, and a skip link.
- Every function works with a keyboard alone; focus is always visible and is
  kept in place when live data refreshes.
- Text and controls meet AA contrast in both Day and Night appearance, and the
  pages respect your system dark-mode, reduced-motion, and high-contrast
  settings.
- Live statistics can be paused, and their changes are not announced
  repeatedly to screen readers; status messages after an action are announced.
- The traffic chart has a text summary and its data is available as a table.
- Form fields have visible labels, instructions, and error messages linked to
  the field.
- Destructive actions ask for confirmation in an accessible dialog.
- Pages reflow down to 320 CSS pixels wide and support 200% text zoom.
